# Clips for the demo video

Nothing is written on these videos, so add your own text in the editor. All 1920x1080, 25 fps, no sound.

- `snippets/`: short pieces, cut at the real moments (grab, hit, drop). Start here.
- `full/`: the whole clip each snippet comes from.
- `screens/`: pictures of the web app and the results table. Their words are part of the app.
- `snippets_overview.png`: one picture of every snippet with its file name.

Clip numbers match `../script.md` and `../edl.md`.

## Rules

- Show "Built with Muse Spark Open" in the video. The model licence requires it.
- Say it is simulation. No real robot was used.
- 02, 03 and 05 use Pexels stock videos. Never say "I filmed" or "my hand". Put `../credits.md` in the video description.
- The 372 of 420 number is for the cube only. Re-plan got 398, so never say only the live loop recovers.
- Clip 22 is your own screen recording of https://thenguyentrong.github.io/skill-translator/
